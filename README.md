\# AI Lead Assistant



An AI-powered lead qualification and management dashboard built to help businesses analyze customer inquiries, identify potential leads, and prepare customer responses.



\## Features



\- Analyze customer inquiries using Google Gemini.

\- Extract service requirements, business type, budget, and timeline.

\- Classify leads by status and priority.

\- Identify missing information and generate lead summaries.

\- Generate suggested responses for customer inquiries.

\- Store lead records in SQLite.

\- View dashboard statistics and recent leads.

\- Search and filter leads by keyword, priority, and status.

\- Open detailed lead records and copy suggested responses.

\- Delete leads with confirmation.



\## Tech Stack



\*\*Frontend:\*\* React, Vite, JavaScript, CSS



\*\*Backend:\*\* Python, FastAPI



\*\*AI:\*\* Google Gemini API



\*\*Database:\*\* SQLite
